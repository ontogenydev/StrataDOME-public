# Design principles

- **Local sovereignty.** Institutions retain authority over protected source state and local policy.
- **No central truth owner.** Shared cognition is distributed, bounded, and attributable.
- **Provenance is structural.** Material claims retain lineage and correction standing.
- **Uncertainty remains explicit.** Unknown, stale, refused, withheld, contradictory, and unresolved states are not collapsed into false certainty.
- **Evidence is not authority.** Epistemic support cannot itself create permission, command, or operational control.
- **Release and acceptance are separate.** A source may release an object without obligating a recipient to accept or act on it.
- **Restrictions propagate.** Sensitive information cannot become less restricted merely because it was transformed, summarized, or routed through another Field.
- **Queries are noncommanding.** A Query requests information; it does not task a source or execute a capability.
- **Fields are emergent.** They are not predefined containers for hazards, organizations, regions, or missions.
- **The lattice is not a sovereign.** Connectivity, scale, rank, or central administration does not confer epistemic or operational authority.
