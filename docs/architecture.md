# Architecture overview

StrataDOME is being developed as a cross-sovereign epistemic protocol for distributed cognition across autonomous domains. Its architecture connects attributable contributions, shared contexts, relational inference and directed inquiry. Participating domains retain distinct authority over their source material and its release, while contributing to reasoning that extends beyond any individual domain’s view.

## Sovereign domains

A domain governs its protected source state, local interpretation and conditions of disclosure. Participation occurs through explicitly permitted contributions. Receiving domains evaluate those contributions within their own context and remain responsible for their subsequent use. Shared reasoning therefore depends on distinct, bounded authorities at both ends of an exchange.

## Signals

Signals are attributable epistemic acts through which a domain makes a contribution available to the wider system. They carry source attribution, interpretive context and uncertainty so that other participants can assess their significance. Their evidential value depends on what they establish, how they were produced and their relationship to other contributions.

## Fields

Fields are emergent shared epistemic contexts constituted from attributable evidence under the rules governing participation. They provide a context in which contributions can be related and assessed together. Their formation requires a justified basis in the evidence; an administrative boundary or a named scenario alone does not establish one.

## Queries

Queries connect unresolved questions to further investigation. They request information capable of distinguishing between competing interpretations, testing a hypothesis or reducing a specific uncertainty. The receiving domain retains authority over its response and disclosure. Returned contributions enter further evaluation, where they may strengthen an inference, require its revision or expose another gap.

## Lattice

The lattice is the evolving structure of relationships among participating domains and Fields. Horizontal relationships support reasoning across domains; relationships across scales support broader interpretations and the return of targeted questions to relevant participants. Authority remains distributed throughout this structure.

## Provenance and restrictions

Provenance connects contributions and derived claims to their sources, transformations and supporting evidence. Applicable restrictions continue to govern downstream use and disclosure; transformation does not itself grant broader permission. Whether a claim remains supported is a separate question, requiring assessment against its evidence, context and conditions of validity.

## Interferometric cognition

Interferometric cognition examines agreement, disagreement and dependence among partial views. Agreement must be assessed against shared sources and derivations before it can count as independent corroboration. Disagreement may reveal incompatible assumptions, differences in context or unresolved uncertainty. These relationships support higher-order hypotheses and generate further Queries, creating a recursive process of observation, comparison, inference and inquiry.
